# 10-Minute Soak Benchmark

The widget left running on a high-throughput live feed: verifying frame rate stability and zero memory, DOM node, or event listener leaks (`pnpm build && pnpm soak` via `scripts/soak.mjs`).

## Test Setup

- **Desk**: Full `ChartWidget` on **1,000,000 one-second bars**, rendered with WebGL 2 at `1600×900` (`DPR = 1`); EMA 21, Bollinger Bands 20, RSI 14, and MACD; paper execution with active position, SL/TP, and three working orders filled/replaced every 15 seconds; bid/ask lines; depth heatmap updated 4×/second; worker feeding **30,000 trades/second** (1 message every 4 ms via `connect({ aggregateTicks: true })`).
- **Environment**: Headless Chrome on Intel UHD 770 (Core i7-14700, 32 GB RAM).

- Heap growth after the first minute: ~0.03 MB/min (~0.3 MB over 9 minutes, matching the 600 newly appended bars and their indicator values).
- Zero main-thread tasks over 50 ms across the 10-minute run (~18 million trades processed; ~70% main-thread idle).
